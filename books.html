The Author

Prof. Joseph Chukwuemeka Abugu, SAN

Prof. Abugu is one of Nigeria's most prolific legal authors, with an impressive body of published works that are standard references in Nigerian law schools and regularly cited by courts at all levels, including the Supreme Court of Nigeria. His textbooks are known for their rigorous analysis, clarity, and practical relevance.

Available for purchase from the firm directly. To order, contact us by phone or email, or visit either of our offices in Abuja or Enugu.
