About the Firm

Nigeria's Premier
Legal Institution

Prof. Joe Abugu, SAN & Co. is one of Nigeria's foremost law firms, founded and led by Prof. Joseph Chukwuemeka Abugu — an esteemed Professor of Law at the University of Nigeria, Enugu Campus, and a distinguished Senior Advocate of Nigeria.

With offices in Abuja and Enugu, the firm combines deep academic insight with practical courtroom excellence, delivering bespoke legal solutions to individuals, corporations, and government entities across Nigeria and beyond.
