Our Story

Distinguished Legal Excellence Since 1995

Prof. Joe Abugu, SAN & Co. was established as a premier legal practice built on the twin pillars of academic rigour and practical courtroom excellence. The firm was founded by Prof. Joseph Chukwuemeka Abugu — a Professor of Law at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka/Enugu Campus, and one of Nigeria's most distinguished Senior Advocates.

Over three decades, the firm has grown into one of Nigeria's most respected legal institutions, advising multinationals, federal and state government agencies, financial institutions, and private individuals on a wide spectrum of legal matters.

With offices in Abuja (the Federal Capital Territory) and Enugu, we are strategically positioned to serve clients across all geopolitical zones of Nigeria and handle matters before every court and tribunal in the country.

Founding & Principal Partner

Prof. Joseph Chukwuemeka Abugu, SAN

Prof. Joseph Chukwuemeka Abugu, SAN, LL.B (Hons), LL.M, Ph.D, FNIALS, FCIArb, is a Professor of Law at the University of Nigeria, Enugu Campus, where he teaches Commercial Law and has supervised numerous postgraduate students to completion.

He was called to the Bar in 1989 and was elevated to the rank of Senior Advocate of Nigeria (SAN) — the Nigerian equivalent of Queens/King's Counsel — in 2009, one of the most prestigious distinctions in the Nigerian legal profession, awarded to counsel for exceptional legal ability and contribution to the law.

Prof. Abugu is a prolific author and legal scholar. His books — including the landmark "Principles of Company Law in Nigeria" and "Secured Credit Transactions in Nigeria" — are standard references in Nigerian law schools and courts. He sits on numerous boards and has appeared as lead counsel in many landmark Nigerian cases.
