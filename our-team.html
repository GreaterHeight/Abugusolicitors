Founding & Principal Partner

Prof. Joseph Chukwuemeka Abugu, SAN

LL.B, LL.M, Ph.D | Senior Advocate of Nigeria

Prof. Joseph Chukwuemeka Abugu, SAN, FNIALS, FCIArb, is the founding and principal partner of the firm. A Professor of Commercial Law at the University of Nigeria, Enugu Campus, and a Senior Advocate of Nigeria (elevated 2009), he is widely recognised as one of the most eminent legal scholars and practitioners in Nigeria.

Prof. Abugu has appeared as lead counsel in numerous landmark cases at all levels of the Nigerian judiciary, including the Supreme Court. He is a prolific author of legal textbooks that serve as standard references in Nigerian law schools and are frequently cited in judicial decisions.

His areas of specialisation include company law, commercial law, secured credit transactions, constitutional law, and energy law. He is a Fellow of the Nigerian Institute of Advanced Legal Studies (FNIALS) and a Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (FCIArb).

Senior Partner

Sir Chima Ubochi

Senior Partner

Sir Chima Ubochi is a Senior Partner with over 25 years of legal experience. He brings exceptional depth of expertise in corporate law, commercial transactions, and high-value litigation before Nigerian courts.

Partner

Bibian Offor

Partner

Bibian Offor is a Partner at the firm with significant expertise in real property law, conveyancing, and banking & finance. She has extensive transactional and advisory experience, having acted in numerous large-scale property transactions and financing arrangements.

Her practice covers the full range of property-related work, from title verification and due diligence through to sophisticated real estate financing structures. She also advises financial institutions on loan documentation and security arrangements.
